Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026

This policy explains how Safe2gether collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Safe2gether mobile application and services.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, username, name, password credentials in protected form, and optional phone number.
  • Social sign-in information: when you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, the provider may give us your provider account ID, name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Safety and location information: location, incident details, guarding-session details, and live location updates when you grant permission and use a feature that requires them.
  • User content and interactions: feed posts, uploaded media, messages, connections, reactions, ratings, and reports.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and deliver the service and its notifications.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide messaging, community feeds, connections, safety alerts, guarding sessions, location-based features, and notifications; prevent abuse; provide support; and improve the reliability of Safe2gether.

How information is shared

Information is shown to other Safe2gether users when the feature requires it or when you choose to share it. We may also share information with service providers that operate Safe2gether, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect a person from harm. We do not sell personal information.

Location and permissions

Location, camera, media, microphone, calling, and notification access depend on device permissions. You may change permissions in Android settings. Turning off a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect users. You may request deletion using our data deletion instructions. Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days; residual backup copies may remain for up to 90 days unless longer retention is legally required.

Security and choices

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. You can update profile information, control device permissions, disconnect social-login access, and request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

Children

Safe2gether is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent.
